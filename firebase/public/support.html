Bragwise Support

Predict. Compete. Brag.

Bragwise is a social prediction game where you create and join prediction challenges, make picks, compete on leaderboards, and connect with friends. This page explains how to get help and answers a few common questions.

Contact us: Email naquden@duck.com and we will get back to you as soon as we can. Please include your username and a short description of the issue (and a screenshot if relevant).

Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in?

Enter your email and we send you a passwordless sign-in link. Open the link on the same device to finish signing in. You can also play as a guest without an email.

I didn't get my sign-in link

Check your spam or junk folder. Make sure the email address is spelled correctly and request a new link. Links expire after a short time, so use the most recent one.

How do I add friends?

Friends find each other by username. Set your username during profile setup, then search for a friend's handle and send a request from the Friends screen.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

Open the Me tab → Notifications, or change it in your device's system settings.

How do I delete my account?

Open the Me tab, scroll to the account section, tap Delete account, and confirm. This permanently removes your data and cannot be undone. Guest accounts are automatically deleted after 90 days of inactivity.
