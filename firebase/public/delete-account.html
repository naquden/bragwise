Delete Your Bragwise Account

App: Bragwise  ·  Developer: Naquden  ·  Last updated: 7 June 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your Bragwise account and the data associated with it, what gets removed, and what is kept (and for how long).

Delete your account from inside the app

  1. Open the Bragwise app and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Tap the Me tab.
  3. Scroll to the account section and tap Delete account.
  4. Read the confirmation and tap Delete to confirm.

This permanently removes your account and cannot be undone. You will be returned to the welcome screen.

Don’t have access to the app?

If you can’t use the in-app option (for example, you signed in as a guest, which has no in-app delete button), email us from the address associated with your account and ask us to delete your data:

naquden@duck.com

Guest accounts that stay inactive for 90 days are automatically and permanently deleted even if you take no action.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your sign-in record, including your email address.
  • Your public profile — display name, username (handle), and avatar.
  • Your player profile, app preferences, and push-notification tokens.
  • Your friends list and head-to-head stats, and your entry in other users' friends lists and pending friend requests.
  • Your predictions and your entries in challenges you joined, including your leaderboard positions in them.
  • Your username reservation and any pending invitations addressed to you.

What is kept, and for how long

DataRetention
Challenges you created Not removed automatically by account deletion, so other participants’ history stays intact. You can delete a challenge you created from its detail screen (before results are posted). Otherwise it is automatically deleted 90 days after results are posted. Challenges that never have results posted are automatically deleted 90 days after they were created.
Security / audit logs Automatically deleted after 90 days.
App data stored only on your device (theme, onboarding state, offline predictions, draft challenges) Removed when you uninstall the app.
